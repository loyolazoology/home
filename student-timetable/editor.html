This is the list the General Timetable picks from. “Staff” are our own department staff (initials). “Other-dept staff” is for people from other departments: type their names, separated by a semicolon (;). They need not be in the staff list. For a common course such as UTL/SN/HN/FR4GL01, add one subject and type the Tamil, Sanskrit, Hindi and French staff there. Changing a subject here does not change slots you have already filled.

Select a slot to choose subjects and staff. Put two subjects in one hour for electives (shown with a /). A lab can be repeated over 2 to 5 hours from the same dialog. Other-department courses and staff, and the common language course, can be added from the same dialog. Saving also rewrites the GeneralTimeTable text sheet and the StaffTT sheet.

Slots filled from the General Timetable update by themselves. Slots you change here are kept and marked “edited”.

A new window opens with the timetables laid out on A4 landscape, one per page, with the Loyola College header. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” as the printer and turn off “Headers and footers”. Allow pop-ups for this site if nothing opens.
